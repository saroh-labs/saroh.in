## How long should a quote be valid for?

Two weeks is common. Make it shorter when your costs change quickly, like the price of materials.

After the date, you can send a new quote at today's prices.

## Should a quote show GST?

If you're registered, yes, so the customer knows the full amount before they say yes. If you aren't registered, you can't charge GST, so leave it off.

## Quotation, estimate or proforma invoice: what's the difference?

A quotation is a fixed price for the work. An estimate is your best guess, and the final bill can differ. A proforma invoice is a quote laid out like an invoice, often sent to ask for an advance.

None of them is a tax invoice, and a customer can't claim GST credit on one. Check with your CA.
