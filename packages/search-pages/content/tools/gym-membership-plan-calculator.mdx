## How big should the yearly discount be?

Big enough that paying for a year is clearly the better deal, and small enough that you still earn more than from members who drop off after a few months.

Many gyms give the year two to three months free, and the 3 months a smaller discount. Keep the yearly discount bigger than the 3 months one, or members will just buy 3 months four times.

## Do I add GST to membership prices?

If your gym is registered for GST, gym and fitness services usually carry GST, and your price list should say whether its prices include it. If you aren't registered, you don't add it.

The rate and whether you need to register depend on your turnover and what you offer, so check with your CA before you print your prices.

## Should I charge a joining fee?

A small joining fee can cover the first assessment and the locker or card, and it makes people think before they sign up. Some gyms drop it on yearly plans to make the year look better still.

Whatever you choose, show it on the price list beside the plans, so nobody is surprised at the desk.
