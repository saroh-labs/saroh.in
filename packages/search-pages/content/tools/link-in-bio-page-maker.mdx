## Where do I put the downloaded page?

Any web host that serves a plain HTML file will do, and several have a free plan. Upload the file, copy the address the host gives you, and paste it in your Instagram bio.

The file holds everything, your photo too, so there is nothing else to upload.

## Can I change my links later?

Yes. Come back, make the page again with the new links, and upload the new file in place of the old one. The address in your bio stays the same.

## How is this different from a website?

This is one page of buttons that sends people somewhere else: your WhatsApp, your booking page, your map. A website has your services, prices and booking on your own pages, and keeps them up to date for you.
