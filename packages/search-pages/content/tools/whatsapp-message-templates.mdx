## Can I send these from WhatsApp Business?

Yes. Copy the message and paste it into any chat, in the ordinary app or WhatsApp Business.

Open in WhatsApp opens the chat on whichever WhatsApp you use on that phone or computer. You press send yourself.

## Can I change the wording?

Yes. Copy the message, paste it into the chat and change any word before you send it.

Keep it short and say who you are in the first line, so the customer knows the message is from you.

## Will WhatsApp block me for sending the same message often?

Sending a booking confirmation or a reminder to a customer who booked with you is the kind of message WhatsApp expects.

What gets numbers reported is sending to people who never asked to hear from you. Message the customers you have, about their own booking or order.
