## What counts as a fixed cost?

A cost you pay every month whether you sell a lot or nothing at all. Rent, salaries, electricity, internet, software and loan payments are the usual ones.

What you spend on each sale, like stock, packing or the UPI fee, goes in the cost per sale instead.

## Should I count my own salary?

Yes, if you want the business to pay you. Put in what you need to take home each month, and the break-even shows the sales that cover it too.

## My sales change by season. Which month do I use?

Use a quiet month to see the sales you need when things are slow, then a busy month to see how far ahead you get. If your costs change by season, change them too.

## What if I sell at very different prices?

Use the average: what you took in a month divided by the number of sales. If one service brings in most of the money, it can help to work it out for that one on its own.
