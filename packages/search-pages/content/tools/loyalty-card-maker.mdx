## How many stamps should a card have?

Eight to ten works for most cafes, bakeries and salons. Fewer stamps for things people buy once a month, more for a daily coffee.

The reward should feel close enough that a regular gets there in a few weeks or months.

## Should the first stamp be free?

Giving the first stamp on the day you hand over the card helps. A card that's already started feels worth keeping.

## How do I stop people stamping their own cards?

Use a stamp with your own mark, or sign each circle with a pen only your counter uses.

When someone claims the reward, take the full card back and tear it.

## What paper should I print on?

Thick card, around 250 to 300 gsm, so it lasts in a wallet. Any print shop can print the A4 sheet on it and cut it for you.

Print the fronts, then put the same sheet back in to print the backs.
