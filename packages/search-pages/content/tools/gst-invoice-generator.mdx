## What must a GST invoice show?

Your name, address and GSTIN, and a number of up to 16 characters that is used once in the financial year. Then the date, the customer's name and address, and their GSTIN if they have one.

For each item it shows the HSN or SAC code, what it is, the quantity, the value, the rate and the tax, with the total and your signature. When the customer is in another state, it names the place of supply. Check the full list with your CA.

## Tax invoice or bill of supply?

A tax invoice is for sales where you charge GST. A bill of supply is for sales where you can't, because you're in the composition scheme or because what you sell is exempt.

Our bill of supply generator makes that one.

## Which rate is a salon service?

Since 22 September 2025 hair, beauty and wellness services like a salon's are at 5%, without input tax credit. Before that they were at 18%.

The rate can depend on exactly what you offer, so check it with your CA.
