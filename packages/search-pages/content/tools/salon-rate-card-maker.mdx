## Should my rate card show prices with GST?

If you're registered for GST, show the price the customer pays, with GST in, and say so on the card. Customers shouldn't find a bigger number on the bill.

Salon services are usually taxed at 18%, but check the rate and whether you need to charge it with your CA.

## Can I show "from" prices for long hair?

Yes. Type the starting price and add the words to the service name, like "Global colour, from" or "Hair spa, long hair".

Say the price for longer or thicker hair at the chair, before you start.

## How often should I update my rates?

Look at them once or twice a year, and whenever your product costs go up.

When you change a price, make a new card and take the old one down the same day.

## Can I add my own services?

Yes. Press Add a service, type its name, price and time, and it goes on the card under its own heading.

Untick any of the common services you don't offer, and they're left off the card.
