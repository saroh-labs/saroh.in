## How much notice should I ask for?

Enough time to give the slot to someone else. For most salons and studios that's 24 hours; for long appointments, like bridal make-up or a colour treatment, 48 hours is common.

For short, walk-in style services, a few hours can be enough.

## Is it fair to keep a deposit?

Most customers accept it when you say so clearly before they book and the deposit counts towards their bill.

Keep it only for late cancels and no-shows, give it back when someone cancels in time, and say both in the policy.

## What do I do when a regular customer cancels late?

That's your call. Many owners let a regular off once, and say so, while keeping the rule for everyone.

Whatever you do, be the same with everyone, so the policy means something.
