## Why doesn't my link show a picture on WhatsApp?

WhatsApp draws the picture named in your page's og:image tag, and only a light one: keep it under 300 KB. Without the tag, or with a heavy file, it shows a small square or none at all.

## What size should the picture be?

1200 × 630 pixels, under 300 KB. Facebook, LinkedIn and X show a smaller picture small or blurred, and WhatsApp skips a heavy one.

## I fixed my tags. Why does the old card still show?

Apps keep a card for a while after they first read your page. Facebook's Sharing Debugger and LinkedIn's Post Inspector read it again on request; the others catch up on their own.

## What does the checker read?

Only the public tags at the top of your page: the title, the description, the picture and X's card type, and the picture's size. It doesn't sign in anywhere, and it doesn't keep your page.

## What happens to my email?

We send the report to it, and keep it with the link you checked for 12 months. We don't send you anything else. The Privacy page has the rest.
