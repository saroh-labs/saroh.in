## Should commission be on sales before or after GST?

Before GST. The GST you collect belongs to the government, not to the business, so paying commission on it means paying your team on money you never keep.

## Flat rate or slabs: which keeps staff happier?

A flat rate is easy to explain and easy to check, which is worth a lot. Slabs reward the people who sell the most, because only the part of their sales above a line earns the higher rate.

Many salons start with a flat rate and add one slab later for their busiest stylists.

## Do I pay commission on a discounted service?

Most businesses pay on what the customer actually paid, after the discount, so the business and the team share the cost of the offer. Whatever you choose, write it down and tell your team before the month starts.
