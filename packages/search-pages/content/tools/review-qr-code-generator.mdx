## Where do I find my Google review link?

Open your Google Business Profile, signed in as the owner, and look for Ask for reviews or Get more reviews. Copy the link it shows. It usually starts with https://g.page/r/.

A link to your Maps listing opens your profile, not the review box, so use the review link.

## Can I check the link before I print?

Yes. Scan the code on screen with your own phone. It should open the review box for your business, with the stars ready to tap.

This tool checks only the link's shape. It can't open your Google profile to see that the link is yours.

## Is it all right to ask customers for reviews?

Yes. Google lets businesses ask customers for reviews.

Don't offer a discount, a gift or money for a review, and ask every customer, not only the happy ones. Google's rules don't allow either.

## Will the code stop working?

The code holds your review link itself, so it keeps working as long as Google keeps that link. If you move or rename your business profile, scan a printed card once to check.
