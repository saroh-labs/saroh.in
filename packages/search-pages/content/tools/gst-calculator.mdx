## When is it CGST and SGST, and when IGST?

When you and your customer are in the same state, the GST is split in half. CGST goes to the centre and SGST to the state.

When the customer is in another state, it's one tax, IGST, at the full rate. Where the supply takes place decides it, and there are exceptions, so check with your CA when you're not sure.

## Which GST rate does my service use?

Since 22 September 2025 most goods and services are at 5% or 18%, and a short list of things like tobacco and luxury cars at 40%. A few keep special rates, like 3% on gold.

Your rate follows your HSN or SAC code. Look yours up, and check it with your CA before you bill at it.
