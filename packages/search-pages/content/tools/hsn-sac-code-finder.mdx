## Do I need HSN or SAC codes on my bills?

If you are registered for GST, your tax invoices usually need them. How many digits depends on your turnover, and bills to customers who aren't registered may not need a code at all when your turnover is under ₹5 crore.

The rules change from time to time, so check with your CA what your bills need.

## How many digits should I use?

Businesses with a turnover up to ₹5 crore usually show 4 digits, and those above it 6. You can always show more digits than you have to, and the longer code says more exactly what you sold.

## My item fits two codes. Which one?

Pick the one that describes what it mainly is, not what it is used for. If you still can't tell, ask your CA, because the code decides the rate and a wrong one can mean paying too little or too much tax.

## Why does one code show two rates?

Some codes cover items taxed at different rates, by the exact item or its price. Clothes and footwear, for example, change rate above ₹2,500 a piece. The finder shows both, and your CA can tell you which one is yours.
