## What's in the file

- Your business name, a line under it and your phone for bookings
- Common services under Hair, Skin, Hands and feet, and Waxing and threading
- A time and a price for each service
- Two empty lines in each section for services of your own
- A filled-in example from a sample salon, with that salon's own prices
