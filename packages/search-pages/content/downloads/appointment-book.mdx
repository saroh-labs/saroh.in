## What's in the file

- Week: Monday to Sunday across, half-hour slots from 9:00 to 21:00 down, and a space for the week's date
- Bookings: one row for each booking, with the date, time, client, phone, service and notes
- Read me: how to start a new week and print it
- The PDF: one week to print and fill in by hand

## A new sheet each week

In Excel or Google Sheets, copy the Week sheet for the next week and write its first date at the top. A service that takes an hour or more goes in each of its slots.
