## What's on the receipt

- Your business name and address, and your GSTIN if you're registered
- The receipt number and date
- Who paid, and their mobile number
- What they paid for, line by line
- How they paid: cash, UPI, card or bank transfer, with the reference
- The amount received, anything still due, and the amount in words
- A space to sign

## A receipt isn't a tax invoice

A receipt records a payment. If you're registered for GST, give a tax invoice for the sale too. The [GST invoice format](/downloads/gst-invoice-format) has one.
