## What's on the invoice

- Your business name, address, GSTIN and state
- The invoice number and date
- The customer's name, address and GSTIN, if they have one
- The place of supply and whether reverse charge applies
- Each line with its HSN or SAC code, quantity, rate and taxable value
- CGST and SGST, or IGST, and the total
- The total in words, and a space to sign

## How the Excel works it out

Type the quantity, rate and GST rate on each line. The taxable value, the tax and the totals fill in by themselves.

When your state code and the place of supply match, the tax is split into CGST and SGST, half the rate each. When they don't, it's IGST at the full rate.

Not sure of a code? Look it up with the [HSN and SAC code finder](/tools/hsn-sac-code-finder).
