## What's on the bill

- Your business name, address and GSTIN
- The bill number and date
- The customer's name and address
- Each line with its HSN or SAC code, quantity, rate and amount
- The total, and the total in words
- The line the rules ask for, and a space to sign

## Which line to keep

On the composition scheme, keep: “Composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax on supplies.”

Selling only exempt goods or services? Write “Supply exempt from GST.” instead.

Charging GST? Use the [GST invoice format](/downloads/gst-invoice-format) instead.
