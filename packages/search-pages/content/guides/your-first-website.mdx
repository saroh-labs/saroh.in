## The four things it needs

Before visiting, people want four answers. Put them where they can't be missed:

| What | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Where you are | Your address, with a map link, so they can find you |
| When you're open | Your hours, and holidays when they change |
| What you offer | Your services or products, with prices where you can |
| How to book or reach you | One clear button: book, call or WhatsApp |

That's a complete first website. Everything else can wait until you know what customers ask for.

## Make it work on a phone

Most people will open your site on their phone, often from a WhatsApp chat or Google Maps. So:

- Your phone number and address should be one tap away.
- Text should be readable without zooming.
- Photos should load quickly. A few good ones beat a big gallery.

## Things that can wait

- A blog or news page.
- A long About page. A few lines about you is enough.
- Every photo you've ever taken.

Add them when you have time to keep them up to date. A page that hasn't changed since last year does more harm than no page.

## Tell people it's there

Put the link in your Instagram bio, your WhatsApp Business profile and your Google Business Profile. Check how it looks when you share it in a chat, with a clear picture and title.

> Your site reads your services, prices and opening hours from where you already keep them. With Bookings on, customers book from the page, and only real free times are offered. See [how to make your link look right when shared](/help/make-your-link-look-right-when-shared).
