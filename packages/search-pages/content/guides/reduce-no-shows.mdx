## Remind them the day before

A short message the day before gives a client the chance to tell you they can't come. Send it yourself in the evening, when people check their phones.

| When | What to send |
|---|---|
| The day before | Your time, the service and how to change it |
| Two hours before | A one-line nudge |

## Ask for a deposit on long services

A deposit makes a booking feel real. Keep it small, and say what happens to it if they cancel:

- **Long services:** a deposit of a quarter is enough.
- **Short services:** no deposit; a reminder does the job.

> **In Saroh** you can ask for a deposit when a client books. See [how bookings work](/help/bookings).

## Make cancelling easy

1. Put your cancel rule in the booking message.
2. Give a number or a link to cancel.
3. Thank people who cancel in time.

## Keep a short waitlist

When someone cancels, offer the slot to the first person on your list. A list of three or four names is enough.
