## Remind them the day before

Most no-shows aren't rude. People forget, or their day changes and they don't think to tell you. A short message the day before gives them an easy way to say so, and gives you a day to fill the slot.

Send it yourself in the evening, when people check their phones. Use the number they booked with, and keep it to two or three lines.

| When | What to send |
|---|---|
| The day before | “Hi Priya, see you tomorrow at 4 pm for your haircut. Can't make it? Just reply and we'll move it.” |
| Two hours before | “See you at 4 pm today.” |

When someone replies that they can't come, thank them. They just gave you time to fill the slot.

## Ask for a deposit on long services

A deposit makes a booking feel real, and it covers part of your loss if someone doesn't come. Ask for it where a no-show costs you most:

- **Long services:** hair colour, bridal makeup, a root canal. A quarter or half is enough.
- **Short services:** a haircut or a check-up. No deposit; the reminder does the job.

Before they pay, say what happens to it: it comes off the bill, it's returned if they cancel in time, and it's kept if they don't come.

> You choose what clients pay when they book each service: nothing, a quarter, half or all of it. It's paid online through your own Razorpay or Cashfree account. See [how to take a deposit when they book](/help/take-a-deposit-when-they-book).

## Make cancelling easy

A client who finds it hard to cancel won't cancel. They just won't come. Make telling you the easy choice.

1. Put your cancel rule in the booking message, for example “Free to cancel up to a day before”.
2. Give one number to call or message to cancel or move.
3. Offer a new time when they cancel, not just a goodbye.
4. Thank people who cancel in time. It's the habit you want.

Need the words for your rule? The [cancellation policy generator](/tools/cancellation-policy-generator) writes one you can copy.

## Keep a short waitlist

When a slot comes free, the quickest person to fill it is someone who already asked for it. Keep a short list of people who wanted a time you didn't have.

- Note their name, number, service and the times that suit them.
- When someone cancels, message the first person who fits. Give them an hour or two to reply, then go to the next.
- Three or four names is enough. A long list goes stale.
