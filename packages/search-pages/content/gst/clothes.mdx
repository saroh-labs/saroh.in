## Each piece, its own rate

A bill with a ₹1,800 kurta and a ₹6,000 lehenga has two rates: 5% on the kurta and 18% on the lehenga. Don't apply one rate to the whole bill.

## Stitching and alterations

Tailoring or alteration done for a customer is a service, billed on its own line at its own rate.
