## Do clinics need to register?

A clinic giving only exempt health care doesn't need to register. If it also sells taxable things, like cosmetic treatments or products, and those cross the limit, it does.

## Bill of supply for exempt care

A registered clinic gives a bill of supply, not a tax invoice, for exempt consultations. Taxable items go on a tax invoice.
