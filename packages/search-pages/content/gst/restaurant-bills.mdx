## Orders through Swiggy or Zomato

For food ordered through a delivery app, the app collects the GST from the customer and pays it, not the restaurant. Your bill to the app shows the food without that GST.

## Service charge

A service charge is not GST and is not compulsory for the customer under the consumer guidelines. If you add one, say so clearly and don't present it as a tax.
