## Memberships, packs and personal training

A monthly plan, a pack of sessions and personal training at the gym are all physical well-being services at 5%. Each renewal or pack sold gets its own invoice.

## What “without input tax credit” means

At 5% you can't take back the GST you paid on rent, equipment or electricity against the GST you charge. Before 22 Sep 2025, at 18%, gyms could claim that credit.

## Supplements and merchandise

Protein, shakers or T-shirts sold at the desk aren't a fitness service. Bill them on their own line, at the rate for their HSN code.
