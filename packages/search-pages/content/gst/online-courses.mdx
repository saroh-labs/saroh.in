## Where the buyer is

For an online course, the place of supply is usually the buyer's state, so a buyer in another state pays IGST. Ask for the buyer's state when they pay.

## Selling to buyers outside India

Export of services can be zero-rated under a letter of undertaking. Recorded courses sold abroad have extra rules.
