## What a tax invoice must show

Your name, address and GSTIN, a number in order, and the date. The customer. Each line with its HSN or SAC, the taxable value, the rate and the tax split. And the total.

## When you're on the composition scheme

Composition businesses don't charge GST to customers. They give a bill of supply that says so.
