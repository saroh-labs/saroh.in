## Take-home or eaten in: two different rates

A cake sold to take home is goods, at the rate for its HSN code. The same cake served at your tables is a restaurant service, at 5% without input tax credit. Bill them as what they are.

## Home bakers

Below the turnover limit for goods you needn't register. Selling through a food-delivery or e-commerce app can make you register whatever your turnover.
