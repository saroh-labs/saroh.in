## What "without input tax credit" means

At 5% you can't take back the GST you paid on rent, products or equipment against the GST you charge. The 5% you collect goes to the government in full.

Before 22 Sep 2025, at 18%, salons could claim that credit.

## Products you sell are taxed on their own

Shampoo, hair oil or a serum sold over the counter isn't a salon service. It goes on its own line with its HSN code and its own rate, not the 5% for services.

## If you aren't registered

Below the turnover limit for services you don't need to register. That limit is ₹20 lakh a year in most states and ₹10 lakh in some. Below it you can't charge GST, so your bill is a plain bill with no tax on it.

A bill of supply is something else. It's for registered businesses with exempt services or on the composition scheme.
