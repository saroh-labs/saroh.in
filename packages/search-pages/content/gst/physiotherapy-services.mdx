## What makes it exempt

The service is treatment, given by someone with a recognised qualification in physiotherapy. A clinic that also sells products or fitness classes bills those separately.

## Exempt but registered

A registered clinic issues a bill of supply for exempt services, not a tax invoice. A clinic that isn't registered gives a plain bill.
