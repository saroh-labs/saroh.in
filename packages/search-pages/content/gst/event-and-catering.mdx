## One bill, two rates

Food served at the event is catering; the stage, flowers and coordination are event management. Bill them on separate lines so each carries its own rate.

## The advance

Caterers and planners usually take an advance at booking. GST on a service is due when you receive the payment, so the advance carries GST when you take it.
