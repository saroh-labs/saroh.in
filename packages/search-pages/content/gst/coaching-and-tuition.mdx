## Who is exempt and who isn't

A school, college or institution that leads to a qualification recognised by law is exempt. A coaching centre preparing students for an exam, or a tutor, charges 18%.

## Books and material

Study material sold with a course may be taxed differently from the coaching itself. Bill it on its own line.
