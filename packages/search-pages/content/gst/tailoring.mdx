## Your cloth or theirs

When the customer brings the fabric, you're selling a service: stitching. When you sell a ready-made piece or the fabric too, that's goods, at the rate for its HSN code and price. Put them on separate lines.

## Small tailors

Below ₹20 lakh a year in services you don't need to register. In some states the limit is ₹10 lakh. Below it you can't charge GST: give a plain bill with no tax on it.
