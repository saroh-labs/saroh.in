## Why yoga and dance differ

Yoga sits with gyms and health clubs as physical well-being, which moved to 5% on 22 Sep 2025. Dance is taught as a skill, so it's usually treated as training and coaching at 18%.

## Yoga by a charitable trust

Yoga run by a trust registered for charitable activities can be exempt. A commercial studio charges 5%.
