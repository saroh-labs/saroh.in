## What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

## Who can read a patient's sensitive notes?

Only you and the people you allow. Allergies still show on a patient's bookings and today's list, so the desk knows to take care.

## Can I give bills without GST?

Yes, mark a treatment as GST-exempt once, and its bills are made without GST. Which treatments carry GST is for you and your accountant to decide.

## How do patients pay me?

By UPI or card when they book or from a payment link, straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account. Cash at the desk works too, and every payment gets a bill.

## Can my front desk use it without seeing everything?

Yes. Give each person a role, and only the roles you choose see the money or sensitive notes.
