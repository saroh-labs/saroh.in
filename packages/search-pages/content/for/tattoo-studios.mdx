## What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

## Can I take a deposit for a sitting?

Yes. Ask for a quarter, half or the full price when they book, paid straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account.

## Can a big piece be booked as several sittings?

Yes, set the piece as several sittings, and tick each one off as it's done. The client books the first sitting, and you book the rest.

## Can a client choose their artist?

Yes. When more than one artist does a service, your booking page offers Anyone or a named person, with only their free times.
