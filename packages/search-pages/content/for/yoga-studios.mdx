## What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

## How do students pay the monthly fee?

Fees can renew by UPI Autopay, set up on your own Razorpay account, or the bill is ready on the day with a payment link to send. Connect your email and each bill goes to them by itself.

## Can I sell a pack of 10 classes?

Yes, sell 5 or 10 classes at one price at the desk. One comes off each time they book, and goes back if they cancel in time.

## Can I run online classes too?

Yes, mark a class as online and add your own Zoom or Meet link. Everyone who books sees it on their booking.
