## What does Start free include?

You can put your website up and start for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

## Can I offer delivery and pick-up?

Yes. Each order says if it's a pick-up or a delivery, with the address, and late ones come to the top.

## Can an office get flowers every week?

Put them on a weekly plan that renews by UPI Autopay, set up on your own Razorpay account, or with a bill and payment link each week. Connect your email and each bill goes to them by itself.

## How do customers pay me?

By UPI or card from a payment link, straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account, or in cash at the counter. Every paid order gets a numbered bill.
