## What does Start free include?

You can put your website up and start for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

## Can I write one bill for a company order?

Yes. Write one bill by hand for the whole order, and see what they still owe, with a payment link ready to send.

## Does each colour keep its own stock?

Yes. Each colour or size keeps its own count, and its own price if you want one.

## How do customers pay me?

By UPI or card from a payment link, straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account, or in cash at the counter. Every paid order gets a numbered bill.
