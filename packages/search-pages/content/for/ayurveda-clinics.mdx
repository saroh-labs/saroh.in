## What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

## Can a therapy run over several days?

Yes, say how many visits it takes, and you see which are done and which are still to book. The patient books the first visit, and your team books the rest.

## Can a patient choose their therapist?

Yes. When more than one therapist does a service, your booking page offers Anyone or a named person, with only their free times.

## How do patients pay?

By UPI or card when they book or from a payment link, straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account. Cash at the desk works too, and every payment gets a bill.
