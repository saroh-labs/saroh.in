## What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

## Can I sell a pack of 10 sessions?

Yes, sell a pack of 10 sessions at one price at the desk. One comes off each time they book, and the pack ends on the date you choose.

## Can a patient choose their physio?

Yes. When more than one physio does a service, your booking page offers Anyone or a named person, with only their free times.

## How do patients pay?

By UPI or card when they book or from a payment link, straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account. Cash at the desk works too, and every payment gets a bill.
