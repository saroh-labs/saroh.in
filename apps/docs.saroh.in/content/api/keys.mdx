## Getting a key

Owners make keys in **Settings › API and AI apps › Keys**. Other people in
the business can't make, see or change keys. Keys come with some plans,
not all.

There are two kinds.

| Kind        | Starts with | Use it                                   | It can                                                                                       |
| ----------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Publishable | `pk_live_`  | On your website, in the browser          | Read what the business's Saroh website shows: products, stock, services, open slots, staff names, posts and plans. Nothing else. |
| Secret      | `sk_live_`  | On your server only, never in a browser  | What you chose when you made it, and never more than you can do yourself.                    |

### When you make a key

- **Name** it after where it's used, like "Shop website" or "Stock sync".
- **Publishable keys** need the websites that may use them. See
  [Storefront keys](/api/storefront).
- **Secret keys** need what they may do, like reading orders or changing
  products. You can only choose what your own role allows.
- **Expiry** is optional. After it, the key stops working.
- **AI apps** is off by default. Turn it on to let a tool that isn't
  Claude or ChatGPT (n8n or Cursor, for example) use this secret key to
  reach the business through the MCP. See [Claude and ChatGPT](/api/ai-apps).

Saroh shows the key once, when you make it. Copy it then. Saroh keeps only
a fingerprint of it, so nobody can show it to you again. The list shows the
first few characters, so you can tell keys apart.

### Using a secret key

Send it as a Bearer token on every call to <code>{API_URL}/v1</code>:

```
Authorization: Bearer sk_live_…
```

Keep it on your server, in an environment variable, and out of your code
and your website.

### Rolling and revoking

- **Roll** a key when you think it may have leaked, or to change it on a
  schedule. You get a new key with the same name, settings and websites.
  The old one keeps working for 24 hours, so you have time to swap it in.
- **Revoke** a key to stop it at once.

### When a key stops on its own

A key acts for the person who made it, as they are now. If their role
changes, the key can do only what both the key and their new role allow.
If they leave the business or are paused, their keys stop: calls get `401`
with the code `key_owner_inactive`. An owner can make a new key.

A key works only on the API address of the Saroh it was made on. Elsewhere
it gets `404`.
