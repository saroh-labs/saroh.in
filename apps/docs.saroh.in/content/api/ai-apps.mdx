# Claude and ChatGPT

Connect Claude or ChatGPT to your business, and ask it to do your Saroh
work: draft a page on your website, add a product, look up today's
bookings, or move one. It connects through Saroh's MCP server at:

<Sample>{MCP_URL}</Sample>

Anyone in a business can connect an AI app. It acts as you, with your
role, so it can do only what you can do in Saroh.

## Connecting

1. In Claude or ChatGPT, add a custom connector and paste the address
   above.
2. Sign in to Saroh when it asks.
3. Pick the businesses it may use: one, several or all of yours.
4. Read what it may do, and agree.

One connection covers every business you ticked. When it covers more than
one, the AI app asks Saroh for the list of them (the `list_businesses`
tool) and names the business on each thing it does (the `business`
argument every tool takes). With one business, it doesn't need to.

What the app reads from Saroh goes to the company that runs it, under its
own terms.

## What it can do

- **Website:** build and change pages, sections, the look, the menu and the
  footer, and add images. Every change is a draft, with a link to preview
  it. Publishing asks you first, and goes to a reviewer when the business
  needs one. You can put the draft back as it was before the AI app's
  changes.
- **Catalogue:** products, services, prices, stock and hours.
- **Day to day:** read, make and move bookings; read, make and change the
  status of orders; read and change customers; read enquiries.

It can't cancel bookings, refund, touch billing or payment settings,
change the team, or close the business. It never sees sensitive customer
details.

Every change it makes is in **Settings › Activity**.

## Staying in control

- **Settings › API and AI apps › AI assistants** lists the connections.
  You see your own. Owners see every connection in the business.
- Open a connection to see each change it made in this business.
- If it changed a website, **Roll back site draft** puts that website's draft back as
  it was before the connection's latest session. The live site doesn't
  change. If someone else has changed the draft since, Saroh won't roll it
  back, so their work isn't lost.
- Disconnect your own connection, or, as an owner, anyone's. This takes
  the business off the connection. The AI app keeps any other businesses
  it covers.
- A connection ends after 90 days without use.
- If you leave the business, or your role changes, the connection follows
  at once.
- ChatGPT asks you to sign in again about once a month.

## Other tools

Tools that sign in with a key instead, like n8n or Cursor, use a secret
key with **AI apps** turned on. Send it as a Bearer token to the same
address. See [Getting a key](/api/keys).
