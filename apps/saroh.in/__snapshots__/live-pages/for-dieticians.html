Questions

What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

Can I do follow-ups on video?

Yes, mark a follow-up as online and add your own meeting link. The client sees it on their booking.

How do plan renewals work?

Plans can renew by UPI Autopay, set up on your own Razorpay account, or each bill is ready on the day with a payment link to send. Connect your email and each bill goes to them by itself.

How do clients pay?

By UPI or card when they book or from a payment link, straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account. Cash at the desk works too, and every payment gets a bill.
