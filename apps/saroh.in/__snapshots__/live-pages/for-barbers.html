Questions

What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

Can I add a walk-in at the counter?

Yes. Add it in a few taps with the barber and the time, and nobody else can book that chair then.

Can a regular choose their barber?

Yes. When more than one barber does a service, your booking page offers Anyone or a named person, with only their free times.

How do customers pay?

By UPI or card when they book or from a payment link, straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account. Cash at the desk works too, and every payment gets a bill.
