Questions

What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

Can a client choose their stylist?

Yes. When more than one stylist does a service, your booking page offers Anyone or a named person, with only their free times.

Can I give GST bills?

Yes. Once you add your GST number, every paid bill is a numbered GST bill with the tax shown.

How do clients pay me?

By UPI or card when they book or from a payment link, straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account. Cash at the desk works too, and every payment gets a bill.

Can my stylists use it without seeing the money?

Yes. Give each stylist a role, and only the roles you choose see the money.
