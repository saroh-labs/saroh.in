Questions

Is a receipt the same as an invoice?

No. An invoice says what a customer owes for a sale. A receipt says you've been paid.

If you're registered for GST, the sale still needs its tax invoice. When you take money before you supply anything, GST has its own receipt voucher for that, so check with your CA.

Can I make a receipt for part of a payment?

Yes. Type what they paid in Amount received, and the receipt shows what's still due.

When they pay the rest, make a new receipt with the next number.

Should I write the UPI reference on it?

It helps. The 12-digit reference in their UPI app matches the payment to your bank statement, and settles any question about it later.

Does a receipt need a signature or stamp?

Not for most payments, but many customers expect your name or signature on it.

A cash receipt for more than ₹5,000 may need a revenue stamp, so check with your CA.

Paid orders mark themselves paid.

In Saroh, a payment is recorded on its order or booking as it comes in, and the customer's copy says it's paid.
