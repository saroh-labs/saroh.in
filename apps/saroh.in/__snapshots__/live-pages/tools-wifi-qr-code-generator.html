Questions

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. The camera app on iPhones and on most Android phones reads Wi-Fi codes and offers to join. On older phones, a QR scanner app or Google Lens does the same.

Print the password on the card too, for the phone that can't.

Is my Wi-Fi password sent anywhere?

No. The code is drawn in your browser and the password stays on this page.

If you leave your email, only the email is sent. The link we email doesn't hold your password; you type it again on this page.

I changed the password. Do I need a new card?

Yes. The password is inside the code, so an old card stops working when the password changes. Make a new code and print it again.

Should guests share the same network as my billing counter?

It's safer not to. Most routers can run a separate guest network. Put guests on that, and keep your billing computer and card machine on your own.
