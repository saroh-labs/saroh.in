Questions

What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

Can a guest choose their therapist?

Yes. When more than one therapist does a service, your booking page offers Anyone or a named person, with only their free times.

Can I take an advance when they book?

Yes. Ask for a quarter, half or the full price when they book, paid straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account.

Can I sell a package of massages?

Yes, sell six massages at one price at the desk. One comes off each time they book, and goes back if they cancel in time.
