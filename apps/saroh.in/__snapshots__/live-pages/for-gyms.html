Questions

What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

Can a member have a class pack and a membership?

Yes. Their page shows the classes left in their pack and when their membership renews.

Can I give GST bills?

Yes. Once you add your GST number, every paid bill is a numbered GST bill with the tax shown.

How do members pay me?

By UPI or card into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account, and memberships can renew by UPI Autopay set up there. Connect your email and each bill goes to them by itself.

Can my trainers use it without seeing the money?

Yes. Give each trainer a role, and only the roles you choose see the money.
