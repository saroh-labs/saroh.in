Questions

What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

How do I collect monthly fees?

Fees can renew by UPI Autopay, set up on your own Razorpay account, or the bill is ready on the day with a payment link to send. Connect your email and each bill goes to them by itself.

Can students book and pay for grading?

Yes, add grading like a class, with its places and fee. Students book and pay, and you have the list before the day.

How do parents pay?

By UPI or card when they book or from a payment link, straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account. Cash at the desk works too, and every payment gets a bill.
