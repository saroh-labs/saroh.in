Questions

What does Start free include?

You can put your website up and start for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

Can I take orders at the counter and on the phone?

Yes. Counter and phone orders go in one list, with how each one is collected or delivered.

Can I give GST bills?

Yes. Add your GST number once, and every paid sale gets a numbered bill with the tax shown.

How do customers pay me?

By UPI or card from a payment link, straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account, or in cash at the counter. Connect your email and each bill goes to them by itself.

Can my staff use it without seeing the money?

Yes. Give each person a role, and only the roles you choose see the money.
