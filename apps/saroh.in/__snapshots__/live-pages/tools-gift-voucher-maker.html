Questions

How long should a gift voucher last?

Six months to a year is common. Long enough that the person who gets it can find a free day, short enough that it doesn't sit in a drawer for years.

Print the end date on the voucher, and keep to it.

How do I stop the same voucher being used twice?

Write each code in a register or notebook when you sell the voucher, and tick it off the day it's used.

This tool makes a fresh code each time, but it doesn't keep a list, so your register is the record.

Do I charge GST when I sell a voucher?

It depends on the voucher. One for a set amount and one for a named service can be treated differently, and the rules have changed in the past.

Ask your CA how to bill the voucher when you sell it and when it's used.

Can I make one for a service instead of an amount?

Yes. Pick A service and type its name, like "Hair spa and blow-dry". The service goes where the amount would be.
