Questions

What counts as a no-show?

Someone who booked and didn't come, without telling you in time to give the slot to someone else.

A cancel so late that the slot stays empty costs you the same, so count those too.

Will customers mind paying a deposit?

People who mean to come rarely mind paying a part up front, as long as you say so when they book and say what happens if they cancel.

Start small, with a quarter of the price, and see how your bookings go.

What if they cancel in good time?

That's your choice, and it's worth writing down. Many businesses give the deposit back, or keep it for the next visit, when someone cancels a day or more ahead.

This calculator counts only the deposits you keep from people who don't turn up.
