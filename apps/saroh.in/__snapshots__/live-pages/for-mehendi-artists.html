Questions

What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

Can I take an advance to hold a date?

Yes. Ask for a quarter, half or the full price when they book, paid straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account.

Can a family tell me how many hands when they book?

Yes. The family can leave a note when they book, and you see it on the booking.

Can I show my designs on my website?

Yes. Add photos of your work to your website, beside each service and its price.
