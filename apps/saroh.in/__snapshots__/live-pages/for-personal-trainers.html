Questions

What does Start free include?

You can set up your website and bookings for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

Can I sell a pack of sessions?

Yes, sell a pack of 12 sessions at one price at the desk. One comes off each time they book, and goes back if they cancel in time.

Can clients book online sessions?

Yes, mark a session as online and add your own Zoom or Meet link. The client sees it on their booking.

Can I set different hours each day?

Yes, set your hours for each day, a break after each booking and your days off. Clients only see those times.
