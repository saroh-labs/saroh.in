How it's written

  1. Each answer adds one short paragraph: how long, in what state, how the money comes back, who pays to send it.
  2. It always says what happens to a damaged or wrong item, even with no returns.
  3. Read it through and change any word. It's a starting point, not legal advice.

Questions

Do I have to accept returns?

You can choose not to take back things a customer simply changed their mind about, as long as you say so before they buy.

An item that arrives damaged, or isn't what they ordered, is different. Customers expect a replacement or their money back, and the policy always says what you do. If you're unsure what applies to your trade, ask a lawyer.

Can I offer store credit instead of a refund?

Yes, for returns you accept because someone changed their mind, if your policy says so clearly before they buy.

For a damaged or wrong item, offer a replacement or the money back rather than only credit.

Where should I show my refund policy?

Where customers see it before they pay: on your website, near your checkout or order form, and at your counter.

If you sell on WhatsApp or Instagram, send it with the order details, so nobody can say they didn't know.
