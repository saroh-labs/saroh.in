Questions cloud kitchens ask.

What does Start free include?

You can put your website up and start for free, with no card needed. Move up to a paid plan when you need more.

I take orders on WhatsApp. Does that still work?

Yes, keep chatting on WhatsApp, and write each order into one list in Saroh. Saroh doesn't send WhatsApp messages for you.

Do my customers get the bill by email?

Yes, once you connect your own email. Each bill then goes to them by itself, with its payment link.

How do customers pay me?

By UPI or card from a payment link, straight into your own Razorpay or Cashfree account, or in cash at the counter. Every paid order gets a numbered bill.
