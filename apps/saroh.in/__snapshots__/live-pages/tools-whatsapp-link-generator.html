How it works

  1. Type your WhatsApp number. Indian mobile numbers only, so the link starts with 91.
  2. Write the message your customer starts with, or pick a start and change its words.
  3. Copy the link and test it on your phone. To put it on your counter, make a QR code of it with the QR code maker.

Questions

Does this work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. The link opens a chat with whichever WhatsApp account uses that number, the ordinary app or WhatsApp Business.

The customer still presses send themselves, so nothing is sent for them.

Where should I put the link?

Anywhere a customer might want to message you: your Instagram bio, your Google Business Profile, your website and the signature of your emails.

On paper, a QR code of the link works better than the link itself, so put that on your visiting card and your counter.

Can I change the message later?

Not on a link you've already shared, because the message is part of the link.

Make a new link with the new message and put it where the old one was. The old link keeps working with the old message.
