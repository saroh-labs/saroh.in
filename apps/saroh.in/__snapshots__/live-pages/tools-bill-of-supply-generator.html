Questions

Who gives a bill of supply instead of a tax invoice?

A seller in the composition scheme, who pays a small share of turnover as tax and can't collect GST from customers. And a registered seller whose sale is exempt, like fresh vegetables or unbranded grains.

The bill shows your name, address and GSTIN, its number and date, the customer, and the HSN or SAC code, description and value of each item. Check the rules for your business with your CA.

Can one bill have exempt and taxed items on it?

Yes. A registered seller can put both on one bill, with GST on the taxed items only.

This tool makes a bill with no GST at all. For a mix, use the GST invoice generator with 0% on the exempt lines, and check with your CA.

Do I need one for a very small sale?

For a sale under ₹200 to someone who isn't registered and doesn't ask for a bill, the rules let you skip it, as long as you make one bill for all such sales at the end of the day.

Many businesses give one anyway, as customers like a bill. Check with your CA.

I'm not registered for GST. What do I give customers?

A plain bill or cash memo, with no GST and no GSTIN on it. Leave the GSTIN empty here and that's what this tool makes.

You can't charge GST until you're registered.
