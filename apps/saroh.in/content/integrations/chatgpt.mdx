**Your website, as a draft.** ChatGPT can build and change pages, sections, the look, the menu and the footer, and add images. Every change is a draft with a preview link. It can't publish: it gives you a link into Saroh, and you choose Publish or Don't publish there.

**Your catalogue.** Products, services, prices, stock and opening hours.

**Your day.** Read, make and move bookings. Read, make and update orders. Read and change customers, and read enquiries.

**What it can't do.** Cancel bookings, refund, touch billing or payment settings, change the team, or see sensitive customer details like health notes.

**Every change on record.** Each change ChatGPT makes is listed under its connection in Settings › API and AI apps, and you can roll its website draft back.
