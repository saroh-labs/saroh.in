{/*
What goes through the business's own provider, from the code:
WHAT: INVOICE_TEMPLATES = INVOICE_SENT, INVOICE_REMINDER (MARKETING_CLAIMS BI2);
TEAM_TEMPLATES = TEAM_ALERT, "a new order, a booking, a failed payment,
someone joining … sent only to the people who chose email for them".
Booking and order notices to customers (NOTICE_TEMPLATES) go only to a live
site account's verified email (site-accounts/notice-reach.ts), and customer
accounts are behind SITE_ACCOUNT_AREA, so they aren't claimed here.
*/}

**Invoices and reminders.** When you send an invoice or a reminder, it goes to your customer from your address, with a link to pay it when you take payments online.

**Alerts to your team.** A new order, a booking, a failed payment or someone joining is emailed to the people on your team who chose email for it.

**Sign-in email stays with Saroh.** Sign-in and security email comes from Saroh, not from your provider.
