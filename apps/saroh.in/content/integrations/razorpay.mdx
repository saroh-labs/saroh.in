**Deposits when someone books.** Ask for nothing, a quarter, half or all of the price when a customer books on your site.

**Pay links on invoices.** Every invoice and reminder can carry a link to pay it by UPI or card.

**Payments matched for you.** When Razorpay confirms a payment, the booking, order or invoice it was for is marked paid on its own. A failed payment or a refund is recorded the same way.

**Plans that can renew on their own.** Memberships and regular orders can renew by UPI Autopay where you've set it up on your Razorpay account. Otherwise each renewal is invoiced with a pay link.
