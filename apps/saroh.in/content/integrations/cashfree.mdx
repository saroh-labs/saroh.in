**Deposits when someone books.** Ask for nothing, a quarter, half or all of the price when a customer books on your site.

**Pay links on invoices.** Every invoice and reminder can carry a link to pay it by UPI or card.

**Payments matched for you.** When Cashfree confirms a payment, the booking, order or invoice it was for is marked paid on its own. A failed payment or a refund is recorded the same way.

**Renewals with a pay link.** Each membership or plan renewal is invoiced on its day, with a link to pay it.
