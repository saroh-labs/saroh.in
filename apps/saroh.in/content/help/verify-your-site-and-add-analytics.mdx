steps:
    # NAV, SECT.
    - title: Open Tracking
      body: "In Saroh, open Website in the sidebar, choose the Settings tab and go to Tracking. What you save here is live as soon as it's saved. You don't need to publish."
      shot: help-verify-analytics-1
      caption: "Search and tracking at Northwind Supply (demo store): its live address, its sitemap and the fields for each search engine's code."
      marker: Tracking
    # VERIFY.
    - title: Verify with Google Search Console
      body: "Under Tracking, open Verification codes. In Google Search Console, add a property and choose URL prefix. Enter your live address exactly as Saroh shows it, then choose HTML tag and copy the tag Google gives you. Back in Saroh, paste it into Google Search Console (the whole tag or just the code) and press Save. Then go back to Search Console and press Verify."
      shot: help-verify-analytics-2
      caption: "Google's tag pasted in at Northwind Supply (demo store), with the code Saroh found in it."
      tip: "Keep the code saved: Google checks it again from time to time. Bing Webmaster Tools works the same way, with its HTML Meta Tag. Meta and Pinterest verify only a domain of your own, so they appear once you've connected one."
      marker: Save
    # VERIFY (sitemap).
    - title: Give Search Console your sitemap
      body: "Your sitemap lists your site's pages, so search engines find them all. Once Google has verified your site, press Copy beside Sitemap, open Sitemaps in Search Console, paste the address and submit it."
      shot: help-verify-analytics-3
      caption: "The live address and sitemap of Northwind Supply's site (demo store), each with Copy."
      marker: Copy
    # TRACK, ASK, PRIV.
    - title: Choose your analytics tool
      body: "Under Your trackers, find the tool you use: Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel, PostHog, Microsoft Clarity, Plausible or Umami Cloud. Press Set up beside it: it says where to find the ID in that tool. If your site has its own privacy page, add its address under Your privacy page."
      shot: help-verify-analytics-4
      caption: "Your trackers at Northwind Supply (demo store), none connected yet, each with Set up."
      tip: "Google Tag Manager and pasted code aren't supported, as they could run anything on your site. Connect each tool on its own."
      marker: Set up
    # TRACK (dialog), ASK.
    - title: Paste the ID and add it to your site
      body: "Paste the tool's ID, or the whole snippet the tool gives you, under ID or snippet. Saroh finds the ID in it and shows it as ID to save; only that is kept. Check it, then press Add to your site. The tool is on your live site at once, and never on checkout or payment pages. To pause it later, use its switch; to change the ID, press Edit."
      shot: help-verify-analytics-5
      caption: "Connecting Google Analytics at Northwind Supply (demo store), with the ID to save shown."
      tip: "Only paste a public ID. Never paste a password or a private key: Saroh refuses anything that looks like one, and doesn't save it."
      marker: Add to your site
    # SITE, ASK, PRIV.
    - title: What your visitors see
      body: "Every tool except Plausible and Umami Cloud sets cookies, so while one is on, your site asks each new visitor first. The notice names your tools, and they run only after the visitor presses Accept. What they do opens your privacy page, or a short page Saroh writes listing your tools. Visitors can change their answer with Cookie choices at the foot of every page."
      shot: help-verify-analytics-6
      caption: "The cookie notice a new visitor sees on the site of Northwind Supply (demo store), with Google Analytics connected."
      tip: "These tools collect data about your visitors for you, so you decide how it is used, as with your customers' other data."
      marker: What they do
