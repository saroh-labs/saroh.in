# The design called these "sizes and options"; the app calls each one a
# variant and the list it picks from an option, so the steps use its words.
# "Sell it at" shows only with more than one location: Rye & Co. has two.
# Nothing was saved: the rows are added to the list and Save variants is
# never pressed (Rye is look-only).
title: Add sizes and options
slug: add-sizes-and-options
area: Products
group: Sell products
order: 20
intro: "When a product comes in sizes, colours or packs, add each one as a variant of it. Each has its own SKU, its own price if it needs one, and its own stock count. This takes about two minutes."
description: "Give a product sizes, colours or packs in Saroh: set up your options, add each variant with its own SKU and price, and choose where each one sells."
readMinutes: 3
updated: "2026-10-10"
publishOn: "2026-10-17"
steps:
    # MORE, OPTS.
    - title: Set up your options
      body: "An option is what customers choose by, like Size, and its values are the choices, like 400g and 800g. In Saroh, open Sell › Products, press More and choose Product settings, then Options. To add a value, press Edit on the option, type the value in the box under its values and press Add value, then Save. You can undo it. For a new kind of choice, press New option, name it, add its values and press Add option."
      shot: help-add-sizes-1
      caption: "Options at Rye & Co. (demo bakery): Size, with 1kg typed in to add."
      tip: "A value a product already uses can't be removed. Change that product's variants first."
      marker: Add value
    # VARI.
    - title: Open the product and press Add variants
      body: "Open the product from Products. Until it has variants it is sold as itself, at its own price. Under Variants, press Add variants."
      shot: help-add-sizes-2
      caption: "The Rye & caraway loaf at Rye & Co. (demo bakery), sold as itself, with Add variants."
      marker: Add variants
    # ADD. Price left blank uses the product's, as the screen says.
    - title: Add each size to the list
      body: "Next to Customers choose by, pick the option this product uses, such as Size. Under Add a variant, pick a value, check the SKU and type a price if this one costs something different. A blank price uses the product's own. Press Add to list, then do the same for the next one. Need a value that isn't there? Manage options takes you back to the list of options."
      shot: help-add-sizes-3
      caption: "The Rye & caraway loaf at Rye & Co. (demo bakery): 800g in the list, 400g with its own price about to be added."
      tip: "If you've set a pattern under Product settings › SKUs, the SKU box suggests one for each variant. Type over it to use your own."
      marker: Add to list
    # SAVE.
    - title: Save variants
      body: "The list isn't saved until you say so: the section is marked Unsaved, with Discard and Save variants at its foot. Check the sizes, SKUs and prices, then press Save variants. You can change a row in place, or remove it with the bin, and save again."
      shot: help-add-sizes-4
      caption: "Two sizes of the Rye & caraway loaf at Rye & Co. (demo bakery), ready to save."
      marker: Save variants
    # ROW.
    - title: Pick a photo and where each one sells
      body: "Press More under a variant. Under Photo shown when someone picks it, choose one of the product's photos. If you sell at more than one location, Sell it at lists them: turn on each location that sells this size. Press Save variants to keep the change."
      shot: help-add-sizes-5
      caption: "The Sourdough loaf at Rye & Co. (demo bakery): the 400g shows its own photo and sells only Online."
      tip: "Each variant has its own stock count. To count and move it, see Count and move stock."
      marker: Sell it at
