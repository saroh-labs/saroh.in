steps:
    # NAV, FORM.
    - title: Open Tax and invoices
      body: "In Saroh, open Settings › Business and choose the Tax and invoices tab. Its rows say whether you're GST-registered and what every invoice carries. On the GST row, press Change."
      shot: help-add-gstin-1
      caption: "Tax and invoices at Northwind Supply (a demo shop), not GST-registered yet."
      marker: Change
    # FORM, GUIDE.
    - title: Switch on GST and type your GSTIN
      body: "In the sheet that opens, turn on GST-registered, then type your 15-character GSTIN. Under the field Saroh splits it into its parts, state code, PAN, entity number, Z and check character, and marks the part that's wrong. The state code sets your state, so you don't choose it separately."
      shot: help-add-gstin-2
      caption: "Northwind Supply (a demo shop) with GST-registered on and its GSTIN in five parts."
      marker: GSTIN
    # ADDR, FORM (addressInTax).
    - title: Check your registered address
      body: "A tax invoice prints your registered address, so Saroh needs its first line, city, PIN and state before it saves the GSTIN. It's on the Registered address tab. If it isn't complete yet, its fields appear in the same sheet, so one Save covers both."
      shot: help-add-gstin-3
      caption: "Northwind Supply's registered address (a demo shop), printed under its legal name."
      marker: Registered address
    # PRINT, TITLE, followRegistration.
    - title: Save, and see a tax invoice
      body: "How it prints, under the sheet's fields, shows your unsaved edit: the paper is now a Tax invoice, with your GSTIN and your state. Press Save. From then on orders and invoices are tax invoices, split into CGST and SGST inside your state, or IGST outside it, with prices including GST. Unless you've set your own invoice number format, it changes to one with the financial year in it."
      shot: help-add-gstin-4
      caption: "How it prints at Northwind Supply (a demo shop): a tax invoice with the GSTIN, not yet saved."
      tip: "Sell only exempt or nil-rated things, like a clinic's treatments? An invoice whose every line is at 0% is printed as a Bill of supply, with no tax columns, in the same number series."
      marker: Tax invoice
    # READY.
    - title: Choose your business type
      body: "Ready to take payments, at the top of Settings › Business, lists what's left before you take money. If you said your business is registered when you set it up, it asks you to choose your business type: private limited, LLP, partnership or another. Press Choose type, pick it in the sheet that opens and save."
      shot: help-add-gstin-5
      caption: "Ready to take payments at Northwind Supply (a demo shop), asking for its business type."
      marker: Choose type
