## Your website

**Website** in the sidebar is where your public pages live.

## Sites and addresses

A site starts on a free Saroh address so you can put it in front of people
immediately, before any domain is set up. You can connect your own domain later
without rebuilding anything.

## What a site is made of

A site is made of **sections** you add to a page — text, images, a list of your
products, an enquiry form, a booking section.

Two of those connect your website to the rest of your workspace, and they are
the reason keeping the site here is worth it:

- An **enquiry form** creates a [contact and a lead](/customers) when someone
  submits it, so an enquiry arrives as work rather than as an email you might
  miss.
- A **booking section** lets visitors reserve a [service](/bookings) themselves,
  which lands straight on your schedule.

## Publishing

Changes are not live until you publish. Until then you can edit freely without
anyone seeing a half-finished page.

## Your site is yours, visually

Your website does not carry Saroh's branding. The workspace you work in and the
site your customers see are styled separately on purpose — your customers should
see your business, not the tool behind it.

## Search engines and your own analytics [#search-and-tracking]

In **Website › Settings › Search and tracking** you can prove to search engines
that the site is yours, and connect the analytics and ad tools you already use.
What you save there is on your live site at once. You don't need to publish.

Every site also has a **sitemap** at `/sitemap.xml` on its address, listing your
pages, posts and (while your shop is open) your products. Search engines find
it on their own; Search and tracking shows the address so you can submit it too.

### Google Search Console [#search-console]

1. In Search Console, add a property and choose **URL prefix**.
2. Enter your site's address exactly as Search and tracking shows it.
3. Choose **HTML tag** and copy the tag Google gives you.
4. Paste it into the Google field in Search and tracking and save.
5. Go back to Search Console and press **Verify**.

Keep the code saved: Google checks it again from time to time. After
verifying, submit your sitemap in Search Console's **Sitemaps** page.

### Bing, Meta and Pinterest [#verify-others]

Each gives you a `<meta>` tag the same way. Paste the whole tag or just the code
into its field. Meta and Pinterest verify a whole domain, so they work once your
site is on your own domain; on a Saroh address only Google and Bing can verify.

### Your own analytics and ad tools [#trackers]

On a paid plan you can connect Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel,
PostHog, Microsoft Clarity, Plausible and Umami Cloud. You enter only the
tool's ID, or paste the snippet the tool gives you and we take the ID from it.
Saroh adds the tool to your pages for you.

- **Visitors are asked first.** Every tool except Plausible and Umami sets
  cookies, so your site shows a short cookie notice and a tool runs only after
  the visitor accepts. Plausible and Umami use no cookies and run without asking.
- **Never on private pages.** Checkout, payment, order and account pages never
  load any of these tools, and what customers type into your forms is hidden
  from session recordings.
- **Never paste a password or key.** Only the public ID goes here. Anything that
  looks like a private key is refused and not saved.
- **No other scripts.** Google Tag Manager and pasted code aren't supported:
  they could run anything on your site. If you need another tool, ask us.

These tools collect data about your visitors for you, so you are responsible
for how it is used, as with your customers' other data. If your site has its
own privacy page, add its address in Search and tracking and the cookie notice
links to it; otherwise it links to a short page listing your tools.

#### Where to find each ID

##### Google Analytics [#trackers-ga4]

Admin › Data streams › your web stream › **Measurement ID**, starting `G-`.

##### Google Ads [#trackers-google-ads]

Tools › Conversions › your conversion › Tag setup › the **conversion ID**, starting `AW-`.

##### Meta Pixel [#trackers-meta-pixel]

Events Manager › Data sources › your pixel › the **Pixel ID**, a long number.

##### PostHog [#trackers-posthog]

Project settings › **Project API key**, starting `phc_`, and whether your project is in the US or EU. Never the personal API key (`phx_`).

##### Microsoft Clarity [#trackers-clarity]

Settings › Overview › **Project ID**.

##### Plausible [#trackers-plausible]

Site settings › your site's script; the ID is the part that starts `pa-`.

##### Umami Cloud [#trackers-umami]

Websites › your site › Edit › **Website ID**.
