## Customers, contacts and leads

Three words that sound alike and mean different things:

- A **contact** is a person. One row per human, however you met them.
- A **lead** is a possible piece of business with that person. One contact can
  have several.
- Your **pipeline** is the same leads on a board, arranged by how far along they
  are.

## Contacts

**Customers → Contacts** is everyone who has enquired, booked or bought.

The columns are chosen to answer *who is worth calling today* without opening
anyone:

| Column            | What it tells you                                            |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Open pipeline** | What is on the table with them right now, and how many leads. |
| **Next booking**  | Whether they are already coming in.                          |
| **Last order**    | What they last spent, and when.                               |
| **Source**        | How they first reached you.                                   |

Sort by **Open pipeline** to see who has the most at stake. Sort by **Last
order** to find people who have gone quiet.

### About "Last order"

Saroh matches an order to a contact when they share an email address, or when
someone has explicitly linked the two records.

So this column can be **empty for someone who has bought from you** — if they
ordered under a different address than they enquired with, we will not guess that
it is the same person. An empty cell means "no order we can confidently attribute
to this person", not "never bought".

We would rather tell you nothing than tell you someone else's purchase.

## Leads

**Customers → Leads** is your open business.

Each row carries what it is worth, how far along it is, and **how long it has
been waiting**. Age is the column most worth watching: a lead sitting at "31 days
waiting" is telling you something a date like "4 July" does not.

Filter by **Open** to see live business, or use the *Open leads* number on Home,
which lands you on exactly that.

Open a lead to change its stage, record its value, write a note, or set a
follow-up task with a due date. **Overdue follow-ups are what put a lead on Home**
— if you want something to chase you, give it a due date.

## Pipeline

**Customers → Pipeline** is the same leads as a board, one column per stage.
Drag a lead across as it progresses.

Use the board when you are thinking about the shape of your business, and the
list when you are working through it. They are the same data.

## Where contacts come from

- Someone submits an enquiry form on your website.
- Someone books a service.
- You add them by hand.

Contacts are deduplicated by email address within your organisation, so a repeat
enquiry from the same person updates the contact you already have rather than
creating a second one.
