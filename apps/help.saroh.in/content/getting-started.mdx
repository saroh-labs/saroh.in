## Getting started

## Signing in

You sign in at [accounts.saroh.in](https://accounts.saroh.in) — with an email
address and password, or with Google or GitHub. That one account gets you into
everything; you never keep a separate password for different parts of Saroh.

If you signed up with an email address, check your inbox for a verification link
before signing in.

## Choosing what your business needs

The first thing Saroh asks is **what your business needs to do** — sell things,
take appointments, follow up on enquiries, be findable online.

Answer honestly rather than ticking everything. Each thing you turn on adds a
section to your workspace and asks for a bit of setup, so switching on all of it
on day one means a long list of chores before you have done any real work. You
can add anything later in two clicks, and nothing you skip is lost.

If you are not sure: pick the one thing you would miss most if it stopped
working this week.

## Your workspace

After that you land on **Home**. It answers one question — what needs you — and
it is worth understanding because it is where you will start most days.

**Needs you** is the ranked work. Each row shows the actual things behind it
rather than only a count, so "Follow up on 5 overdue leads" lists which five,
who they are with, how much each is worth and how late it is. You can decide who
to call without opening anything.

The ranking is deliberate:

| Badge         | Means                                                    |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Attention** | Something you rely on has stopped working.                |
| **Overdue**   | Someone outside your business is waiting.                 |
| **Setup**     | Something is on but not finished. It can wait.            |
| **Suggested** | A nudge. Ignore it freely.                                |

Overdue sits above Setup on purpose. An unfulfilled order is a person waiting;
finishing a configuration step is not.

**Coming up** shows your next bookings, grouped by day, each in the timezone it
was booked in — so a customer's 9am shows as the 9am they chose, wherever they
are.

**Your numbers** sits at the bottom rather than the top. Every one of them is a
link to exactly the rows it counts, so "14 open leads" opens your leads already
filtered to the open ones.
