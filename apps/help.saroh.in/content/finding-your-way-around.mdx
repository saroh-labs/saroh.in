## Finding your way around

## The sidebar

The sidebar is named for the work, not for where data is stored. You will only
see the sections you have turned on.

| Section           | What lives there                                    |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Home**          | What needs you today.                               |
| **Sell**          | Your stores, products and orders.                   |
| **Bookings**      | Your schedule, and the services people can book.    |
| **Customers**     | Contacts, leads and your pipeline.                  |
| **Website**       | Your pages and how they are published.              |
| **Insights**      | How things are going.                               |
| **Notifications** | Things that happened while you were away.           |
| **Settings**      | Your organisation, team, capabilities and providers. |

An **amber number** beside a section means work is waiting there — five overdue
leads, five orders to fulfil. It is the same count Home is ranking, so the two
can never tell you different things. No number means nothing is waiting; that is
not a bug.

## Search — the fastest way anywhere

Press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows) anywhere, or click Search in the top bar.

Type a customer's name, a lead, or an order number. You can type a **full name** —
"ananya rao" finds her as readily as "ananya" does. Results are grouped by what
they are, and Enter opens the highlighted one.

The same box also starts things ("New service", "New store") and jumps to any
section, so it is usually quicker than reaching for the sidebar.

## Every list works the same way

Contacts, leads, orders, products, services and bookings all behave identically
once you have learned one of them.

### Three densities

Top-right of every list:

- **Table** — the most information at once. The default on a computer.
- **Grid** — cards with pictures. Good for products.
- **List** — one line per row. The default on a phone.

Your choice is remembered **per list**, so you can keep Contacts as a table and
Products as a grid, and it will still be that way tomorrow.

The phone view is not a cut-down version — it is the same information at a
different density, so nothing is hidden from you because you are not at a desk.

### Filters

The chips above each list are named subsets, each showing how many rows it holds:
*Open pipeline*, *Have bought*, *Needs fulfilling*, *Unconfirmed*.

Choosing one changes the address bar, so you can bookmark a filtered view or send
it to someone on your team and they will see the same rows.

If a filter turns up nothing, the list says so *and* tells you how many rows
exist in total — an empty "Unpaid" means good news, not a broken screen.

### Sorting and searching

Click a column heading to sort. Click again to reverse it. Click a third time to
clear it — you can always get back to where you started.

The search box above a list narrows the rows you are looking at. It is not the
same as ⌘K: this searches the list in front of you, ⌘K searches everything.

## Making it look how you want

In the top bar:

- **The palette icon** switches between visual styles. Purely your preference;
  it changes nothing about your data or who can see it.
- **The sun/moon icon** switches light and dark.

Both are remembered in the browser you set them in, so your phone and your
laptop can differ.
