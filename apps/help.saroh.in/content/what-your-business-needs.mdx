## Turning things on and off

Saroh is built from capabilities you choose. **Settings → Modules** is where you
change your mind.

## Adding something later

Turn it on and it appears in your sidebar immediately. Most things then ask for
one setup step before they can do new work — a payment provider, an availability
window — and Home will tell you which.

There is no penalty for starting small. Adding selling to a business that began
with bookings takes two clicks and keeps everything you already have.

## Turning something off

Turning a capability off does two things:

- It **leaves your navigation**, so you stop seeing sections you do not use.
- **New activity stops** — no new bookings, no new orders, depending on what you
  switched off.

**It does not delete anything.** Your existing data stays exactly as it is, and
turning the capability back on shows it again, unchanged. Saroh will say this in
the confirmation before you do it.

If something cannot be turned off, it is because another capability you have on
depends on it. The dialog will name which.

## What each capability gives you

| Capability         | You get                                                    |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sell**           | Stores, products, orders and store customers.              |
| **Bookings**       | Services, availability and a schedule.                     |
| **Customers**      | Contacts, leads and a pipeline.                            |
| **Website**        | Public pages, enquiry forms and booking sections.          |
| **Insights**       | How things are going.                                      |
| **Payments**       | Taking money online. Needs a provider connected.           |
| **Communications** | Sending messages. Needs a provider connected.              |

## Why your workspace looks smaller than someone else's

Because you asked for less, and that is the intended outcome rather than a
limitation. A business that only takes bookings should not have to look past
products and orders every day to reach its schedule.
