## Bookings

Two things under **Bookings** in the sidebar:

- **Schedule** — what is actually booked.
- **Services** — what people can book.

## Services

A service is something someone can reserve: a consultation, a fitting, a
walkthrough.

It carries a **duration**, a **capacity** (how many people can take the same
slot), an optional **price**, and a **timezone**. In the table these are columns
rather than a sentence, so two services can be compared at a glance.

Only **Bookable** services can be reserved. Use that filter to check nothing you
meant to offer is quietly switched off.

### Availability is the step people forget

A service with no availability windows **cannot be booked**, however correct
everything else about it is. Setting availability is what turns a service from a
description into something a customer can actually take.

Until you do, Bookings shows a "Set your availability so customers can book"
item on Home. That is the one Setup item worth doing early, because unlike the
others it is the difference between taking bookings and not.

## Your schedule

**Schedule** lists what is coming up, nearest first, grouped by day. The next
couple of days read as **Today** and **Tomorrow** rather than dates, because
those are the two that change what you do next.

Filters: **Today**, **Next 7 days**, and **Unconfirmed** — the last is the one
to check, because an unconfirmed booking is someone who thinks they might be
coming.

### Times are shown in the timezone they were booked in

If someone in London books your 9am, you see the 9am they chose, with the
timezone stated beside it. Saroh does not silently convert times into yours,
because the number your customer wrote down is the number that matters when you
call them.

### Cancelling

Confirmed bookings have a **Cancel** button on the row — on your phone as well as
your computer. Cancelling frees the slot so someone else can take it.

## Who booked

When the person booking matches someone you already know, the row shows their
name and links to their contact record, so you can see the rest of their history
before they arrive.

A walk-in booked without contact details shows as *Unknown booker*. That is a
real state, not a fault.
