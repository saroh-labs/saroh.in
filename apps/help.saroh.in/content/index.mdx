## How can we help?

Saroh is one place to run your business — sell, take bookings, follow up on
enquiries, and keep your website in step.

You only get the parts you ask for. If you never take appointments, nothing about
appointments appears anywhere in your workspace.

### Start here

- [Getting started](/getting-started) — your first sign-in, and choosing what
  your business needs.
- [Finding your way around](/finding-your-way-around) — the sidebar, search, and
  how every list works.

### The work

- [Customers, contacts and leads](/customers) — who has enquired, and who to
  call today.
- [Selling](/selling) — your store, products and orders.
- [Bookings](/bookings) — services, availability and your schedule.
- [Your website](/website) — pages, publishing and your address.

### Settings

- [Your organisation and team](/organisation) — details, people and roles.
- [Turning things on and off](/what-your-business-needs) — adding a capability
  later, or switching one off.
