## Your organisation and team

An **organisation** is your business inside Saroh. Everything — contacts, orders,
bookings, sites — belongs to one, and people you invite get access to that one.

If you run two genuinely separate businesses, use two organisations and switch
between them from the top bar. Do not use two for two parts of the same
business; you will spend your life switching.

## Organisation details

**Settings → Organisation** holds your business name and details.

Your organisation's **web address cannot be changed after it is created** — links
already shared with customers would break. Choose it deliberately at the start.

## Your team

Invite people by email address. Each gets a role:

| Role       | Can do                                                                 |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**  | Everything, including deleting the organisation.                       |
| **Admin**  | Run the business day to day, and manage people. Cannot delete the org. |
| **Member** | Read-only: see the organisation, stores and sites.                     |

**Members deliberately cannot see contacts, leads or customer details.** That
information is personal to the people who gave it to you, so it is limited to
owners and admins rather than being available to anyone with a login.

Pick Admin for people who run the business with you, Member for people who need
to see it but should not hold customers' details.

## Your own account

Your name, email address and password belong to **you**, not to any one
business — so they live in your account at
[accounts.saroh.in](https://accounts.saroh.in), not in organisation settings.
That way one login carries across every business you belong to.

Organisation settings hold the business; your account holds you.

## Providers

**Settings → Providers** is where you connect outside services — a payment
provider to take money, a messaging provider to send emails.

Each shows what state it is in:

| State             | Means                                                       |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Connected**     | Working.                                                    |
| **Not connected** | Nothing set up yet. Nothing is wrong.                       |
| **Not working**   | It was connected and has stopped. Worth looking at now.     |

Only **Not working** is a problem. A provider you have never set up says
"Not connected" and offers "Set up" — it is not broken, you just have not got to
it yet.

When something a capability depends on stops working, it shows on Home with an
**Attention** badge, which is the highest rank there is. That is the one to act
on the day you see it.
