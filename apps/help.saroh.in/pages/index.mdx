## How can we help?

This is the help center for **Saroh** — the platform to create blogs, portfolios, and storefronts.

Here you’ll find guides on:

- **Dashboard** — Creating and managing your sites, content, and settings
- **Pricing** — Plans, limits, and upgrades
- **Account** — Profile, security, and billing

Use the sidebar to jump to a topic. If you’re building or contributing to the Saroh codebase, see [docs.saroh.in](https://docs.saroh.in) instead.
