## How a claim is judged

- **Backed**: a merchant can do it today with the module on and their own
  provider connected (Razorpay or Cashfree for money, their own email
  provider for messages).
- **Partly**: some of it is true. The row says what is missing.
- **Not backed**: not built, or built but switched off in production with
  no date.
- **Fixed**: the copy was reworded or removed (2026-10-03) and now says only
  what is Backed. The last column holds the words that shipped.

Two kinds of switch decide many rows:

1. **Module rollout flags** (`MODULE_*` in `feature-flags/flags.ts`) fail
   closed and are turned on per business. They are launch configuration,
   not a gap, so they don't lower a status here. At Gate O every module a
   page names must be on for a new business.
2. **Dark features**: built and tested, but off in production until a
   release turns them on. A claim that rests on one is **Partly** (or
   **Not backed** when nothing else holds it up) until the switch is on:

| Switch                       | Where                                                                   | What waits for it                                                                         |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `SITE_SHOP` flag             | `orders/public-checkout.service.ts`, the renderer                       | Your online shop: products on the site, bag and checkout, public reviews                  |
| `SITE_ACCOUNT_AREA` env      | `site-accounts/account-area.ts`, `saroh.app/lib/account-area-switch.ts` | Customer accounts: move or cancel a booking, join a plan, pause, buy a pack, later visits |
| `ACCOUNT_THREAD` flag        | `site-accounts/notice-reach.ts`                                         | Messages in the customer's account                                                        |
| `RAZORPAY_AUTOPAY` flag      | `payments/providers/razorpay-mandates.ts`                               | UPI Autopay / card mandates; until then every renewal is an invoice with a pay link       |
| `SITE_TEST_RELEASES` flag    | `sites/test-releases.*`, `sites/publish-approval.ts`                    | Test releases and "Publishing needs approval" (advisory review is live without it)        |
| `CLOUDFLARE_HOSTNAMES_*` env | `domains/domain-hosting.provider.ts` (off when unset, WARN at boot)     | Your website on your own domain: registering it and its certificate (CAP1)                |
| U13 plan enforcement         | not on `development` (branch `cat/u13`)                                 | Any per-plan limit the catalogue names, apart from sites, locations and own domain        |
